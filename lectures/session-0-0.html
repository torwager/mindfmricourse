Day 1 · Acquisition, preprocessing, and the GLM

Course introduction

Introduces the instructors and the scope of the three-day course: paradigm design, image acquisition, and analysis of fMRI data. Sets up the two complementary software packages used throughout, SPM (model-based, univariate GLM) and GIFT (data-driven, multivariate ICA), and states what participants should take away from each session.

Scroll down

Take-aways

  • The course covers design, acquisition, and analysis, not analysis alone.
  • SPM (GLM) and GIFT (ICA) are complementary approaches and both will be used hands-on.
  • Aim for roughly 10 concrete take-home points from each session.

Key terms

  • SPM
  • GIFT
  • General Linear Model (GLM)
  • Independent Component Analysis (ICA)
  • model-based vs. data-driven
  • univariate vs. multivariate
  • paradigm design

Outline

What the session covers

01Who and what

  • Instructors: Kent Kiehl (Mind Research Network / UNM), Vince Calhoun (TReNDS; Georgia State, Georgia Tech, Emory), Tor Wager (Dartmouth).
  • Three themes run through the course: paradigm design, data acquisition, and analysis.
  • Framed as everything you wanted to know about fMRI but were afraid to ask.

02Two complementary software packages

  • SPM (SPM8/12) implements the General Linear Model: model-based and univariate, one voxel at a time.
  • GIFT implements Independent Component Analysis: data-driven and multivariate.
  • The course gives a practical, hands-on introduction to both, with lectures and tutorials interleaved.

03Goals of the course

  • Hands-on overview of fMRI image acquisition and analysis.
  • From each lecture or tutorial, aim to take about 10 points home.
  • Those points should improve how you review the fMRI literature and how you design and run your own experiments.
  • Day 1 follows the SPM processing pipeline from raw images through preprocessing to the GLM.
