How it works

Three steps to enroll

  1. Register and payClick the button for your category above. You will be taken to a PayPal-hosted checkout page for the course fee. A receipt is emailed to you when payment completes.
  2. Complete the short questionnaireAfter paying, please fill in the post-registration questionnaire so we can tailor the course to attendees' backgrounds and send you the right materials.
  3. Watch for course materialsBefore the course you will receive the calendar invitation, the Zoom link and passcode, the Dropbox link for data and tools, and a MATLAB trial license.

Questions

Need help?

Enrollment is first-come, first-served and is limited by the interactive nature of the course. For questions about registration, eligibility categories, or invoices, contact kkiehl@mrn.org.
